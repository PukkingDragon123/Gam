How to Play

The Duel

You and your opponent trade turns. The attacker commits a direction. The defender moves their head.

Same direction → HIT. Different → DODGE.

It's a four-way mind game: don't be where the punch lands.

Controls

Head: lean up / down / left / right.

Hand: move your open hand in front of the camera.

Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D.

Hold a direction to lock it in. Stay centered and you'll get hit.
