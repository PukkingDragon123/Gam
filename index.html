How to play

✋ Hand controls

  • Move hand left / right → steer the board
  • Raise hand → jump over waves
  • Lower hand → crouch under banners
  • Open palm → speed boost
  • Closed fist → sharp turn

No webcam? Keyboard works too — see below.

⌨️ Keyboard

  • ← → / A D — steer
  • ↑ / W / Space — jump
  • ↓ / S — crouch
  • Shift — boost
  • Q / E — sharp turn left / right

🌊 The vibe

  • Collect floating music notes.
  • Thread glowing rings to build a combo.
  • Find hidden islands for fan facts.
  • Surf a full day → night cycle.
  • It's cozy: wipeouts cost combo, never your run.

Calibrate your hand

Hold one hand up, comfortably centered in the frame, fingers relaxed. Then calibrate — that becomes your neutral steering position.
